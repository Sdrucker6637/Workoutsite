# Exercise illustrations

These illustrations come from **Everkinetic** open data: <https://github.com/everkinetic/data>.
They were created by Greg Priday (everkinetic.com), and the dataset is maintained with Frank Paczynski and other contributors.

**License:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The full text is in `LICENSE.md`.

**Changes made for Forge:**
- The original `NNNN-relaxation.svg` and `NNNN-tension.svg` files were renamed to `NNNN-a.svg` and `NNNN-b.svg`.
- The files were minified with SVGO. This changes their size only, not how they look.
- Each frame's viewBox was cropped to the drawn figure, with a small margin. Both frames of a pair share the same crop.
- In the app, the two frames alternate to form a looping animation.

These adapted files are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. The mapping from Forge exercises to illustrations is in `js/media.js`. Every pair was checked by eye, and exercises without an exact match show a placeholder instead.

The Exercise Gym GIFs DB (github.com/JahelCuadrado/ExerciseGymGifsDB) was evaluated and **not used**. Its own README states that its GIFs were collected from the internet, that the author does not hold their copyright, and that no rights can be granted to third parties.
