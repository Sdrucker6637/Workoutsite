# Branded nutrition-data coverage

Generated by `node tools/coverage.js --write` on 2026-11-23. The app shows the same numbers live (Settings → Nutrition → Branded coverage), including products you've saved yourself.

A **branded product** here is one product line or variant in the brand catalog (`js/brands.js`) — something you can name, such as "Dave's Killer Bread Good Seed Thin-Sliced". Flavors with their own label are counted separately below.

- **Complete** — Forge holds protein, calories, carbs and fat, the label serving size and a named source.
- **Partial** — Forge holds the label but at least one value wasn't on any source found. It is left blank (shown as "not listed"), never guessed.
- **Without nutrition** — recognized, but no label on this device. Forge asks which product, then offers online search or label entry; it never borrows another product's numbers.

## How these values were collected

This build environment could reach a web search service but not the manufacturer, retailer, USDA or Open Food Facts pages directly. The Phase 4 values were therefore read from search-result summaries of the pages linked below, not from the pages themselves, and were cross-checked across listings. That is why most new labels are **Reference data** rather than Verified, and why every conflict is kept as an Estimate with a note. Re-check a product against its package (My foods → edit → *Last checked*) to make it yours.
